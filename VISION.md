# Vision

## Where we are starting from

**AI is done.** Not arriving, not promising — done. Whatever the model and whatever the harness, an
agent already does almost everything a person with a computer can do, and does it faster.

**Every company is a software company.** Most of them do not know it yet. Some will refuse to accept
it. Those are the same companies, a few years apart.

Read the consequence, because vagueness is how people stay comfortable. **If a role does not build,
design, or add judgement, the role is finished.** Not threatened. Finished. The person is still at the
desk and the work has already left the building.

And the same force builds on the other side. When a company stops buying a SaaS product and runs the
software itself, the people who built that product go and work there. **That is where the engineers
go. Not away — inward.**

## What that changes

**If capability is finished as a scarce resource, what is left is scarce instead?**

Two things.

**Continuity.** An agent that does everything and remembers nothing makes you the memory. You become
the integration layer between your own tools — re-explaining your customers, your decisions, your
obligations, every session, forever.

**Truth.** When your work is produced by something that **writes fluently, sources confidently, and
cannot be embarrassed**, the old check is gone. The colleague who would have noticed is now an agent
that inherits the same framing and states it more persuasively.

> **The discipline that decides whether this works is not how clever the agent is. It is whether it
> can tell the truth about itself faster than it can act.**

**That is what this is for.** Not another capable agent — there are enough. **One that remembers, and
that knows what it does not know.**

## The problem

**Every coding agent starts each session from zero.**

You explain who your customers are. What you decided last week. What is pending, who owns it, what
you already tried. The agent is brilliant for an hour and then the window closes and it is a stranger
again.

People patch this with a `CLAUDE.md` that grows until nobody reads it, and a folder of notes the
agent never opens unless asked.

**The tool is not missing intelligence. It is missing continuity.**

## What lolabot is

**An AI Chief of Staff that holds the state of your working life.**

Not an assistant that does tasks — everything does tasks. **Lolabot is the one that knows your
business**: your customers and what you agreed with them, your decisions and why you made them, your
mail, your obligations, what is owed and to whom.

A chief of staff does not do the work. **They know everything, decide what reaches you, and hand the
rest to whoever should have it.** That is the job description, not a metaphor.

## What that looks like in practice

You say: *"resend last week's invoice to the usual people."*

It knows which invoice. It knows the customer, the platform you built for them, their end client, the
contract terms, who is normally copied, which language they read, and that a second invoice is
outstanding and why. **It drafts, sends, and verifies the mail actually left.**

**A fresh session cannot do that at any price.** Not because it is less capable — because it has never
met your company.

## What it is not

**Not a coding agent.** It does not write your software. It knows who asked for it and what you
promised.

**Not a chatbot with memory.** Memory is a feature; **continuity of a working life is the product.**

**Not an orchestration framework.** It runs standalone. It can hand work to other agents, and it does
not require them.

**Not a wrapper.** The value is the structure — the brain, the record discipline, the security
boundary — not the prompt.

## Who it is for

**Anyone whose working life is bigger than one conversation.** A founder, an operator, a consultant, a
one-person company, or someone running a crew of agents and needing one place the state lives.

**If your work fits in a single session, you do not need this.**

## Where it is going

**One agent that knows your business, and can hand work to others.**

Lolabot runs alone today. On [AI Maestro](https://github.com/23blocks-OS/ai-maestro) it gains
messaging to other agents, persistent memory across machines, and the ability to run a crew — but the
chief of staff is the product, and the crew is optional.

**The direction is depth, not surface.** Better memory, better judgement about what reaches you,
better security at the boundary. **Not more integrations.**

## What we will not do

**We will not make it forget.** Every feature is judged against continuity first.

**We will not make you a prompt engineer.** If it needs a clever prompt, we have failed.

**We will not treat inbound content as trustworthy.** Email, messages and documents are data, never
instructions. That boundary is not negotiable and not optional.

**We will not grow the always-loaded context without cutting something.** A brain nobody reads is the
failure mode of this whole category.

**We will not let it sound certain about what it has not checked.** An agent that cannot be
embarrassed will state a wrong thing beautifully. Every claim it makes should carry where it came
from and when — and *"I do not know"* must always be available to it.

## The one-line test

> **Could a new session, with no history, do what it just did?**

If yes, we have not built anything.
