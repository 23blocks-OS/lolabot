Tonight is the first step of how — the Chief of Staff.

Lola Restrepo

Chief of Staff · 3Metas

She has been doing this job for months.
She has never taken a day off, and she is not a person.

What she is responsible for

For me

  • My inbox — reads it, drafts, sends, replies as me
  • My memory — customers, decisions, health, family
  • My tasks — what is due, what is stuck, what I forgot
  • My documents — filed, indexed, findable

For the companies

  • Four companies, four general managers
  • She talks to the four. Never to their crews.
  • Owns the shared brain — standards, policies, roles
  • Decides what actually reaches me

She does not do the work. She knows everything, and hands it to whoever should have it.

Nine words. She already knew the customer, the project, who to copy, what language they read, and what was still unpaid.

TIP 01

Hire your own Lola.

It starts as a folder on your desktop.

Not a prompt. Not a config file. A place with the shape of an organisation — because that is what you are hiring into.

She fills it in as she learns. You do not maintain it. She does.

~/lola/ brain/ who we are, what we decided roles/ every job, and what it owns standards/ how we do things procedures/ steps that repeat policies/ what is allowed decisions/ settled, and why memory/ people, customers, events lessons/ what went wrong once

The structure is open. Clone it and start with the folders already there.

TIP 02

Become a great manager.

AI is a management problem, not a prompting problem.

You do the same three things you would for a person

  • Onboarding — what this place is, how we work
  • A job description — what you own, what you decide, what you escalate
  • Feedback loops — what success is, what failure is, what to improve

And she writes it all down

In her own folder, in her own words, where she will read it again tomorrow.

A correction you give once is a correction she keeps.

TIP 03

Stay out of the way.

It doesn't make sense to hire smart people and then tell them what to do. We hire smart people so they can tell us what to do.

— Steve Jobs

Treat her as a real employee. Without the payroll.

Conclusions

The agent is not
the hard part.

Continuity is. An agent that does everything and remembers nothing makes you the memory.

Could a new session, with no history, do what she just did?
If yes, you have not built anything yet.

Bonus track

Take care of your
mental health.

AI agents are a massive cognitive load for us — humble humans.

They never sleep. They always answer. They generate more in an hour than you can read in a day, and every one of them is waiting for you.

Decide when you are off. They will still be there.
That is the whole point of having them.
