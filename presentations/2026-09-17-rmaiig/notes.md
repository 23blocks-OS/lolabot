## 1 · The elephant in the room — 45s

**Verbatim opening:**

> Let me start by addressing the elephant in the room: **I don't speak English.** If you are
> understanding anything of what I'm saying, it is because your Spanish is getting great, your brain
> is flexible, and now it is adapting in real time and trying to understand the way that I speak.

**Then the pivot — this is the actual thesis of the talk:**

> The reason I am starting with that is because I believe **good ideas are obvious**, and once you
> learn one it feels as natural as gravity. So my hope is that in this quick conversation **the hard
> part will be my accent, not the ideas.**

*Do not rush the pause after the first line. Let them laugh.*

## 2 · Where I am starting from — 60s

Six theses, one reveal each. **Do not read them.** Say the one underneath:

**AI is not a tool.** A tool waits to be picked up. This does not.

**It already does 99.99% of human work better than any human.** Say it plainly and let it sit.

**I stopped caring about models, harnesses and frameworks.** They change every month. What you do
with them does not — and that is the only part worth learning.

**Land on:** this is not a technology, it is a way of thinking. Everything after this slide is a
consequence of it.

*If running long, this is where to cut — theses 05 and 06 can go.*

## 3 · Headcount — 30s

Nine bars barely off the floor, then one that fills the screen. **Let the chart do the work.**

*"Nine years to eleven people. Nine months to a hundred and thirty-six."*

Then: tonight is the first step of how — and the first step is not the org. **It is one chief of
staff.**

## 7 · Demo — 90s

The largest block. **The invoice.**

Nine words in. She knew: which invoice, the customer, the platform, their end client, who is
normally copied, that they read Spanish, and that a second invoice was outstanding — and that
"unpaid" was really "never received."

**The line that matters:** a fresh session cannot do that at any price. Not because it is less
capable. **Because it has never met your company.**

*Have a screenshot ready in case live fails. Never debug on stage.*

## 8 · Tip 1 — Hire your own Lola — 45s

**It starts as a folder.** Not a prompt, not a config file — a place with the shape of an
organisation, because that is what you are hiring into.

Point at the tree. **The rule that makes it work: she fills it in, not you.** A brain you maintain by
hand is a document. A brain she maintains is a memory.

## 9 · Tip 2 — Become a great manager — 45s

**AI is a management problem, not a prompting problem.**

The same three things you would do for a person: onboarding, a job description, feedback loops —
what success is, what failure is, what to improve.

**Close on:** a correction you give once is a correction she keeps.

## 10 · Tip 3 — Stay out of the way — 40s

The Jobs quote, then the real content:

**Stop prompting.** Stop telling it the steps. Give it the request, and let it decide what to do
based on the procedures you wrote and your definition of success.

**If you are still writing prompts, you are still doing the job.** Treat her as a real employee —
without the payroll.

## 11 · Conclusions — 20s

The agent is not the hard part. **Continuity is.**

**Close on the question:** could a new session, with no history, do what she just did? If yes, you
have not built anything yet.

## 12 · Bonus track — 40s

**Do not introduce it. Just show it.**

They never sleep, they always answer, they generate more in an hour than you can read in a day, and
every one of them is waiting for you. **Decide when you are off.**

*This is the slide people will remember. Slow down.*
